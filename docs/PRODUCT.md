# Альфа-Ромео — product context

## Product purpose
A concept demo of a 2035 retail + business bank for Russia (iOS, SwiftUI). One identity, several
profiles (личный / бизнес / семейный / детский), one money layer (₽, цифровой ₽, крипта, ЦФА),
own MVNO, AI copilot. Full spec: `docs/Alfa-Romeo_Master_Spec.md`.

## Users and scene
A person in Moscow checking balances, paying and transferring on the go: one hand, metro carriage
or street, daylight or harsh office light, a few seconds per glance. A small-business owner doing
the same between meetings. Investors and partners watching a live demo on a big screen.
Light theme is primary (daylight glance), dark is a supported mode.

## Brand
Heir of a big Russian bank: red is the heritage colour, used sparingly and with confidence.
Tone: precise, calm, factual, adult. A bank that knows the numbers, not a startup that sells a mood.

## Anti-references (what it must NOT look like)
- "AI made this": sparkle ✨ icons, gradient-bordered "insight" banners, blue→cyan fintech
  gradients, glow shadows, pastel tinted icon tiles on every row, identical card grids,
  everything wrapped in a rounded white card.
- Marketing copy inside the product: slogans in subtitles, em-dash taglines, "· · ·" chains.
- Crypto-bro neon, glassmorphism, 3D coins.

## References (the bar)
Apple Wallet and Settings (structure, restraint), Revolut / Monzo (number typography, density),
Linear (precision, one accent), Swiss banking print (grid, hierarchy by scale and weight).

## Principles
1. Numbers first: balances and amounts are the largest, cleanest things on screen.
2. One accent: red marks the primary action and current selection, nothing decorative.
3. Structure over containers: grouped lists and whitespace, not stacks of cards.
4. Every word earns its place: labels are nouns, subtitles carry data, no slogans.
5. Live data is labelled honestly (источник, время, «демо» when it is a demo).
